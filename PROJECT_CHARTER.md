# CTS Research Hub — Public Project Charter

Updated November 9, 2026.

Provide a readable, low-friction research hub for LSU Health New Orleans PCCM faculty, fellows, residents and students. Preserve direct institutional links, title-case headings with appropriate small words and scientific names, the purple/gold presentation, exact audience banner, existing primary navigation, and working conference and grant forms.

Preserve the original 40 dataset records and unchanged Word directory. The prepared online directory adds six verified resources for 46 datasets in 18 topics. External registries are not LSU-owned databases; access remains controlled by their providers.

Conference scheduling remains the second and fourth Wednesdays at noon America/Chicago, with private emails, explicit display consent, booking-conflict rules and token cancellation. Homepage display wording is 2nd & 4th Wednesdays @ noon. Do not invent presenters, location or holiday exceptions.

Lowenstein PCCM Micro-Grants remains the primary funding option. Keep the short application, private-save-first workflow and working native HTML email handoff. Do not add application burden, grant policy or awards without approval. Keep other LSU Health intramural programs subordinate in a collapsed section below the form/recipients. The new deadline table excludes national grants and conferences, labels historical calls and does not imply unverified current availability.

Project Examples should connect feasible design to valid conclusions, with accurate status, author approval and faculty review. No real examples have been supplied. Worksheets are educational aids, not approved protocols. Additional upload functions, Grant Application category, example population and access-control expansion are deferred.

Use this repository's main branch and the existing research-site deployment. Regenerate browser data with input changes. Preserve private data and credentials; do not force-push divergent history or alter unrelated sites. Verify tests and actual production before claiming a release is live.
